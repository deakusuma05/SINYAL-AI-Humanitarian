Module 1 • System Architecture & Operational Scope

Humanitarian Decision-Support Triage Overview

Language Scope: English / Translated-English Messages Only

Purpose & Human-in-the-Loop Workflow

During high-volume disasters, humanitarian volunteers must screen thousands of incoming messages containing aid requests, general situational updates, weather reports, and offers of assistance. SINYAL is a lightweight, reproducible supervised NLP screening tool designed to organize incoming English (or English-translated) messages for human reviewers.

05 Human Review Queue Verified by volunteers; zero auto-deletion
Strict Operational Boundaries (What SINYAL Does Not Do):
  • Does not autonomously dispatch emergency personnel or allocate physical supplies.
  • Does not diagnose medical conditions, determine clinical urgency, or verify request authenticity.
  • Does not discard or delete unflagged messages; all low-score messages remain in routine monitoring.

    Offline Held-Out Benchmark (n = 2,387)

    Chart 1: Per-Label Held-Out Test F1, Precision & Recall (τ*)

    Scientific Interpretation: Core physical needs achieve strong held-out test F1 (food: 0.8129, water: 0.8104, request: 0.7404, shelter: 0.7277). In contrast, medical_help achieves F1 = 0.4917 (Precision 0.4906, Recall 0.4929) due to heterogeneous clinical symptoms and public-health reporting vocabulary in disaster text.

    Offline Held-Out Benchmark (n = 2,387)

    Chart 2: Validation-Frozen Thresholds (τ*) vs. Default (τ = 0.50)

    Why F2 & Macro F1 Matter: Under severe class imbalance (positive rates 4.5%–20%), default τ = 0.50 misses over 41% of aid requests and 81% of medical needs. Tuning request for F2 (τ* = 0.19) weights recall twice as heavily as precision, raising held-out Request F2 from 0.6284 to 0.7781 and Macro F1 from 0.6113 to 0.7166.

    Exploratory Subgroup Audit • Held-Out Test (n = 2,387)

    Chart 3: Source Genre Robustness Comparison — Direct Crisis Reports (n = 1,067) vs. News Media (n = 1,320)

    Critical Subgroup Finding: On direct citizen/SMS reports (where 39.46% of messages are true aid requests), SINYAL achieves Request F2 = 0.8492 (Recall = 89.07%, F1 = 0.7937, Macro F1 = 0.7484). On news reports (where only 4.32% are requests and needs are described in third-person narrative), Request F2 drops to 0.1880 (Recall = 17.54%, Macro F1 = 0.5559). This confirms SINYAL is best suited for direct message streams rather than journalistic articles.

    Module 6 • Scientific Honesty, Operational Boundaries & Risk Disclosure

    Known Limitations & Humanitarian AI Governance

    Mandatory Pre-Deployment Disclosure
    01

    English / Translated-English Scope Only

    The model is trained and evaluated strictly on English-language disaster messages and English translations from the Munro corpus. It does not parse regional idioms without translation.

    02

    Indonesian Language Not Validated

    Performance on untranslated Bahasa Indonesia, regional Indonesian languages (e.g., Javanese, Sundanese), or mixed code-switched social media posts has not been validated by this benchmark.

    03

    Historical 2010–2012 Dataset Domain Shift

    The benchmark originates from historical crises (primarly 2010 Haiti earthquake, 2010 Pakistan floods, 2012 Superstorm Sandy). Contemporary messaging platforms and modern crisis terminology may exhibit domain shift.

    04

    Lower medical_help Performance (F1 = 0.4917)

    Held-out test F1 for medical_help is 0.4917 (Recall 49.29%, Precision 49.06%). Bag-of-words TF-IDF struggles with diverse symptom descriptions and indirect medical references.

    05

    Direct vs. News Genre Disparity

    While direct SMS/citizen messages achieve Request F2 = 0.8492 (89.07% recall), third-person news messages achieve only Request F2 = 0.1880 (17.54% recall).

    06

    Residual False Negatives (56 Unflagged Requests)

    On the held-out test set, the human-review policy captured 422 of 478 true requests (88.28%) by flagging 33.93% of messages, but 56 true requests (11.72%) remained unflagged due to implicit or brief phrasing.

    07

    Mandatory Human Verification Policy

    Model-estimated scores are uncalibrated probabilities (mean Brier = 0.0495) and never represent clinical urgency or verified truth. Every flagged item requires human review.

    08

    Not an Autonomous Dispatch System

    SINYAL is a research decision-support prototype for information organization. It must never be used to automatically deploy responders, ration aid, or reject unflagged cries for help.
